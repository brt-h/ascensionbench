Terms of use: Ascension Bench overlay (Twitch extension)

The service. The Ascension Bench overlay is a free Twitch video-overlay extension for the channel twitch.tv/ascensionbench. It shows information about the Slay the Spire game on screen (cards, relics, potions, the map, powers and intents) as tooltips and panels over the video. It is provided by Ascension Bench, an unofficial project not affiliated with Mega Crit Games or Twitch.
