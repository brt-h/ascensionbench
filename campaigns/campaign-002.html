Notes on the campaign

  • Execution. Launched 2026-09-20 03:25 UTC from the committed manifest (manifest.json, 120 entries interleaved across configurations and seeds) at concurrency 12 with a $120 spend guard; the campaign finished in 16.4 hours at $71.50 of model spend with no stale heartbeat and no machine left behind. Every replicate reached an end: 96 deaths, 10 victories and 14 action-budget stops, all of the budget stops Luna's. Machine time over campaign and reruns: 200 machine hours (139 machine runs, performance-2x with 4 GB, about $18 at Fly's list price). Phase 1 model spend including the reruns: $78.56 (spend.json); 139 machines launched and destroyed (cleanup-proof.json, machines-after.txt).
  • Reruns. The 14 budget-stopped Luna replicates were rerun once from manifest-reruns.json (2.1 hours, $7.07): ten ended in deaths and now score; four stopped at the action budget again (E05 replicate 2, E08 replicate 1, E13 replicate 2, E20 replicate 1) and stay unscored, so Luna has 36 scores over 20 seeds, 16 of them with both replicates. One rerun (E10 replicate 2) ended in a harness error, StateTimeout: state never settled after 425 actions (the game's state stopped changing between reads, the first such case in 139 runs), and the runner's own infrastructure retry of it ended in a death that scores.
  • Provider incidents. DeepSeek returned 503 Service is too busy and 504 Provider timed out after 301 s in a window around 08:00 to 10:00 UTC; four first attempts (E09, E11, E14 and E16, replicate 1) were cut by it after the client's six retries, and the runner's one infrastructure retry of each reached a natural end, so no DeepSeek replicate is unscored. The harness waits out a 429 for up to fifteen minutes but treats a 503 or 504 as a failed call after about two minutes of backoff; that rule was not changed during the campaign because it lives in the harness hash. Nothing else failed: every request was served by the pinned first party under the listed model name (verification.json, verification-reruns.json, 139 of 139 run directories pass the v8.1 checks).
  • Invalid replies. DeepSeek: 101 of 24,415 requests (0.41 percent), of which 32 ran to the 32,000-token cap while reasoning (finish_reason: length) and 17 came back empty with finish_reason: error at zero cost; GLM: 40 of 15,549 (0.26 percent), all format errors; Luna: 19 of 51,880 (0.04 percent), including 3 empty error replies. No configuration is near the five-percent defect line.
  • Loops. Luna's shop loops recurred across the campaign: 33 of its 40 scoring runs contain loop steps, 11,593 of their 30,019 steps in total, and the 18 budget stops (14 in the campaign, 4 in the reruns) each carry 636 to 1,342 loop steps. DeepSeek (51 loop steps in 6 runs) and GLM (25 in 4 runs) barely loop. The loop column is defined in the per-run table's caption.
  • Two readings of the tables. The between-seed standard deviation is estimated at zero for DeepSeek: its two replicates on a seed differ as much as its runs on different seeds do (within-seed SD 12.4 floors), so on these seeds the model's own variance, not seed difficulty, dominates its spread; GLM's spread splits evenly (7.7 and 7.7). One DeepSeek victory (E08 replicate 1) is recorded at floor 47 as the game reported it (a VictoryRoom in act 3 after Awakened One); the other nine victories are recorded at floor 51.
